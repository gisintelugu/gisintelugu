About GIS in Telugu

Welcome to GIS in Telugu! We are dedicated to making Geographic Information Systems (GIS) technology accessible to Telugu-speaking communities across the globe.

Our mission is to break the language barrier in technical education by providing high-quality GIS tutorials, courses, and resources entirely in Telugu. We cover a wide range of topics including QGIS, ArcGIS Pro, ArcGIS Online, GeoServer, AutoCAD, and more.

This platform is designed for students, researchers, government employees, and anyone interested in mapping and geospatial technology who prefers learning in their native language.
